Luma: Lost Islands — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 October 2026 · Publisher: Burak Uluzmanoğlu (App Store), albay19 (Google Play) · Contact: uluzman47@gmail.com

Summary

Luma is an offline puzzle game for iPhone, iPad and Android. It does not collect, transmit or sell personal data. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or real-money purchases.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, scores, unlocked islands and buildings, the current puzzle, and language and sound preferences are stored only in the app's storage on your device. The developer never receives these records. They remain until you delete the app or its data.

Permissions and network

The game makes no network requests. It does not access contacts, photos, location, the camera or the microphone. The only link that leaves the app is this privacy policy, which opens in your browser.

Backups

On iPhone and iPad, the save may be included in the device backup (iCloud or computer) that you control through Apple; the developer has no access to it. On Android, system backup is disabled and the game keeps a local copy of the save on the device.

Third parties

The game includes no advertising, analytics or payment SDKs and shares no data with third parties. Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) may process information under their own policies when you download, review or update the app.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Contacting the developer

If you write to uluzman47@gmail.com, your message and address are used only to answer you and are kept only as long as needed. You can ask for their deletion at any time.

Changes

This policy will be updated before any change to these practices, such as adding online features, advertising, analytics or payments.
